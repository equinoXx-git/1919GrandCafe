Our Heritage

A Century of
Timeless Elegance

Nestled within the historic walls of the former Hongkong & Shanghai Banking Corporation building on Juan Luna Street, 1919 Grand Cafe breathes new life into one of Binondo's most architecturally significant landmarks.

Originally erected in 1922, this neoclassical masterpiece features soaring Corinthian columns, ornate ceiling reliefs, and grand marble floors — meticulously restored to preserve the grandeur of Old Manila while offering a contemporary culinary and social experience for today's discerning guests.

Every visit to 1919 Grand Cafe is more than a meal — it is a journey through time, an immersion into heritage, and an invitation to create lasting memories in the heart of the world's oldest Chinatown.

"To dine here is to feel the soul of Manila — where century-old walls whisper stories of merchants, bankers, and dreamers who shaped Binondo."

— The Grand Cafe Heritage Foundation
